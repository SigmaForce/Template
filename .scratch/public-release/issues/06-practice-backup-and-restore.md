# 06: Practice Neon backup and restore

**What to build:** documented seven-day recovery posture, daily encrypted
off-provider logical backup, and repeatable restore drills.

**Blocked by:** 02, 04.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- Backup encryption, access, retention, and ownership are explicit.
- Restore is rehearsed into an isolated environment with synthetic verification.
- Restore does not overwrite a live environment automatically.
- Recovery time and data-loss assumptions are recorded honestly.
